01 / MULTIMODAL HRI

Alliance AI Lab · JHU

Ongoing research

A cognitive layer for safer human–robot interaction

How can human intent inform a robot’s safety-aware behavior?

I am developing Tri-level Human-Centered Safety Filter (Tri-HCSF), adding a cognitive layer to the Human-Centered Safety Filter (HCSF). My work combines fine-tuned vision-language models with haptic feedback and visual and voice cues.

Vision + voice/Cognitive layer/Haptic interaction
02 / SURGICAL ROBOTICS

LCSR · JHU

Seeing and learning surgical interaction

How can richer perception support remote control and autonomous manipulation?

Digital-twin VR teleoperation

I co-developed a system for the da Vinci Research Kit (dVRK) that combines markerless perception, a digital twin, and multiple spatial views for surgical robot control.

Related manuscript ↓

Force-aware imitation learning

I built simulation and data collection pipelines in AMBF, extended Action Chunking with Transformers (ACT) with force inputs, and evaluated the approach for autonomous surgical manipulation.

03 / AI FOR HEALTH

Intuitive Computing Lab · JHU
NeuroAgent AI

Making health experiences easier to understand

How can conversations and sensing support a clearer picture of health?

Conversational tools for sleep health

Working with sleep specialists, I developed data processing and clinician-facing analytics for LLM-powered sleep diaries. I also contributed to DREAM (Discovery, Reflection and Education for Awareness and Management of Sleep), a voice assistant for guided reflection and personalized sleep education.

Sleep diary & visualization preprint ↗

Computational sensing for neurology

At NeuroAgent AI, I contribute to computational sensing and computer vision research supporting precision neurology and digital biomarker initiatives.
